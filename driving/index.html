The Dual Lens双重镜头

The same road, experienced from two completely different risk profiles. This is the empathy that autonomous driving engineers need.

同一条路，从两种完全不同的风险视角去体验。这是自动驾驶工程师所需要的共情能力。

🚗 AS A DRIVER作为驾驶员

  • I know the blind spots where e-bikes hide我知道电动车隐藏的盲区在哪里
  • I anticipate the sudden swerve — rider checking phone, one hand on the bar我能预判突然的转向——骑手在看手机，单手扶把
  • I feel the stress of "any mistake could kill someone"我承受着"任何失误都可能导致有人死亡"的压力
  • I have internalized Chinese "scrambling" right-of-way logic我已内化中国式"争抢"路权逻辑
  • I can detect lane-cut intent 2-3 seconds before it happens我能在加塞发生前2-3秒察觉到意图
  • I understand why Chinese drivers honk — it's communication, not aggression我理解中国司机为什么按喇叭——是沟通，不是攻击

🛵 AS AN E-BIKE RIDER作为电动车骑手

  • I know what it feels like when a car "doesn't see you" at an intersection我知道在路口被汽车"没看见"是什么感觉
  • I've been forced off the road by right-turning cars that never checked mirrors我被不查看后视镜就右转的汽车逼出过道路
  • I understand why riders weave — potholes, glass, and parked cars that cars don't notice我理解为什么骑手要绕行——坑洼、碎玻璃、汽车注意不到的违停车
  • At UCSB, I commuted 6 km daily — bike lanes, roundabouts, campus traffic在UCSB，我每天骑行6公里——自行车道、环岛、校园交通
  • In China, I dodged delivery riders who run reds while checking phones在中国，我躲避边看手机边闯红灯的外卖骑手
  • I am the Vulnerable Road User that autonomous cars are programmed to protect我就是自动驾驶汽车被编程要去保护的弱势道路使用者

"You don't truly understand a near-miss until you've been on both sides of it. As a driver, it's a jolt of adrenaline. As a rider, it's your life flashing before your eyes. That difference — that asymmetry of consequence — is what every autonomous driving system must internalize."

"直到你从双方的角度都经历过差点碰撞，你才算真正理解它。作为驾驶员，那是肾上腺素飙升的一瞬间。作为骑手，那是你一生的画面在眼前闪过。这种差异——这种后果的不对称性——是每个自动驾驶系统都必须内化的。"

From Dreamer to Defender从追梦者到防御者

A lifetime on wheels, leading to one mission: making autonomous driving safer for everyone on the road.

一辈子的轮上生活，通向一个使命：让自动驾驶对路上每个人都更安全。

CHILDHOOD童年
The Dream
那个梦想
Fascinated by cars. Not just speed — control. Understanding how a machine responds to human input. Dreamed of the day I'd hold a steering wheel.
对汽车着迷。不只是速度——是操控感。理解机器如何响应人类的输入。梦想着有一天握上方向盘。
2019
Perfect Exam Run
驾考满分通关
All four PRC driving exam subjects passed on first attempt within 2 months — far faster than the national average. Subject 2 (parking skills) and Subject 3 (road test) with full marks.
中国驾考4个科目2个月内一次性通过——远快于全国平均水平。科目二（场地技能）和科目三（道路驾驶）满分。
2020–2024
The Hardening — Northern Chinese Urban Driving
淬炼——中国北方城市驾驶
Daily driving in aggressive northern Chinese traffic. Cut-ins at centimeter distances. E-bikes from blind spots. Delivery riders on phones running reds. Developed pre-cognitive hazard detection. ~1-2 near-misses per driving day forged an instinct no textbook can teach.
在中国北方激烈交通环境中每日驾驶。厘米级加塞。盲区中窜出的电动车。外卖骑手看手机闯红灯。形成了预认知级别的危险探测能力。每天1-2次差点碰撞，锻造了教科书无法教授的直觉。
2021–2025
The Rider Years — UCSB + China E-bike
骑手岁月——UCSB + 中国电动车
4 years at UCSB: 6 km daily bicycle/e-bike commute through campus, roundabouts, and California roads. Learned the vulnerability of being invisible to cars. Returned to China and experienced e-bike chaos from the handlebars. This completed the feedback loop: I now knew both what drivers miss and what riders fear.
UCSB 4年：每日6公里自行车/电动车通勤，穿越校园、环岛和加州道路。学会了"被汽车忽视"的脆弱感。回到中国后在电动车上体验混乱交通。这完成了反馈循环：我现在既知道驾驶员会忽略什么，也知道骑手害怕什么。
NOW现在
The Mission
使命
34,000+ total km on wheels. Zero accidents. A brain trained as a human sensor fusion system — processing driver, rider, Chinese, and American perspectives simultaneously. Ready to apply this to the hardest problem in autonomous driving: making FSD work in the world's most complex traffic environment.
34,000+公里总轮上里程。零事故。一个被训练成人肉传感器融合系统的大脑——同时处理驾驶员、骑手、中国、美国四个维度的视角。准备好将其应用于自动驾驶最难的问题：让FSD在世界上最复杂的交通环境中工作。
